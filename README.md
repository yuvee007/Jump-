2D Platformer Game
Description

Welcome to the 2D Platformer Game!
Navigate through exciting levels filled with challenging obstacles, enemies, and hidden secrets. Jump, dodge, and collect power-ups to advance and unlock new stages. Enjoy smooth controls and classic platforming gameplay designed for players of all skill levels.

How to Play

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move your character.

Press the jump button (spacebar or up arrow) to jump over obstacles.

Collect coins and power-ups to enhance your abilities.

Avoid enemies and hazards to survive.

Reach the end of each level to progress.

Features

Multiple levels with increasing difficulty

Various enemy types and obstacles

Power-ups to boost gameplay

Hidden collectibles and secrets
